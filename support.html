Mission Express App Support

Welcome to the official support page for Mission Express Logistics Inc. apps. This page provides support information for Mission Rush and Mission Go.

Supported Apps

Mission Rush

Mission Rush is the customer app for requesting delivery services, creating orders, managing account information, and viewing order progress.

Mission Go

Mission Go is the driver and operations app for handling delivery tasks, parcel pickup, cargo pickup, and related logistics workflows.

Before Contacting Support

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include the following information when contacting support:

  • App name: Mission Rush or Mission Go
  • App version
  • Device model
  • iOS version
  • Order number or tracking number, if applicable
  • Screenshots of the issue, if available

Response Time

We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.
