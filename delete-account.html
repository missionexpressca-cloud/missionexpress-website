Mission Go Account Deletion Request

If you would like to request deletion of your Mission Go account and associated personal data, please send your request by email to info@missionexpress.ca.

Please include enough information for us to identify your account, such as your registered email address, phone number, and full name.

What to include in your request

  • Your full name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your phone number used for the account
  • A short statement that you want your Mission Go account deleted

What happens next

After receiving your request, we will review it and process account deletion within a reasonable time, subject to verification and any legal or operational record-retention requirements.

Data retention

Certain information may be retained for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or operational compliance purposes where required by law or legitimate business needs.

Contact

If you have any questions about account deletion or privacy, please contact info@missionexpress.ca.
