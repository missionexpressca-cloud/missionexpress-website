Mission Express Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Mission Express app account. It applies to both Mission Rush and Mission Go.

Mission Rush

Mission Rush is the customer app used to request delivery services, create orders, manage account information, and view order progress.

Mission Go

Mission Go is the driver and operations app used to manage delivery tasks, parcel pickup, cargo pickup, and related logistics workflows.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account, please contact Mission Express Logistics Inc. by email using the information below.

Email Request

Please send your account deletion request to:

Email: info@missionexpress.ca

Email Subject: Account Deletion Request

Suggested Email Content:

Hello Mission Express Support,

I would like to request deletion of my Mission Express app account.

App name: Mission Rush / Mission Go
Full name:
Registered phone number:
Registered email address:
User ID / Driver ID / Account ID, if known:

Please process my account deletion request.

Thank you.

You may copy the email address, subject, and suggested content above into your preferred email app.

Open Email App

If the button does not open, please manually send an email to info@missionexpress.ca with the subject “Account Deletion Request”.

Information to Include

To help us locate and verify your account, please include the following information in your request:

  • App name: Mission Rush or Mission Go
  • Your full name
  • Your registered phone number
  • Your registered email address, if applicable
  • Your user ID, driver ID, or account ID, if known
  • A short statement confirming that you want your account deleted
Important: For security reasons, we may need to verify your identity before processing an account deletion request.

What Will Be Deleted

After your request is verified and approved, we will delete or deactivate personal account information associated with your app account, where deletion is legally and operationally permitted.

This may include:

  • Account profile information
  • Login access
  • Saved contact information
  • Saved addresses, where applicable
  • App account preferences

Information We May Need to Retain

Some information may be retained when required for legal, tax, accounting, fraud prevention, safety, dispute resolution, refund processing, or delivery operation records.

This may include:

  • Completed order records
  • Payment and refund records
  • Invoices, receipts, or transaction history
  • Driver delivery records, where applicable
  • Records required by law or business compliance obligations

Retained records will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

Processing Time

We aim to respond to account deletion requests within 1–2 business days. The full deletion or deactivation process may take additional time depending on account verification, active orders, payment records, or legal retention requirements.

Before Requesting Deletion

Please make sure that:

  • You do not have active delivery orders in progress
  • You have saved any information you may need later
  • You understand that deleted account access may not be recoverable
