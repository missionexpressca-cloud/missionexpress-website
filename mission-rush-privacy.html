Mission Rush is a same-day and on-demand delivery service platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you use Mission Rush:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, phone number, and login credentials.
  • Address information, including pickup and drop-off addresses, saved addresses, and contact details for senders or recipients.
  • Order information, including item type, delivery notes, order history, payment status, and delivery preferences.
  • Payment-related information, such as stored payment method references, wallet top-up history, and transaction records. We do not store full card numbers in our app systems.
  • Location information, if you grant permission, to help determine pickup points, improve address accuracy, and support delivery-related features.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Customer support communications, if you contact us by email, phone, or other support channels.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process delivery requests and related payments.
  • Provide order tracking, notifications, and customer support.
  • Maintain account balance, wallet top-up, and payment method functions.
  • Verify identity, reduce fraud, and protect the security of users and transactions.
  • Improve app performance, service quality, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

3. Location Data

If you allow location access, Mission Rush may use your device location to help you enter addresses more accurately, identify relevant service areas, improve pickup and drop-off experience, and support delivery-related functions.

You may control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may limit certain app functions.

4. Payment Information

Payments and digital wallet features may be processed through third-party payment providers, including Square and related payment services. Sensitive payment credentials are handled by the payment provider in accordance with its own security and compliance standards.

Mission Rush may store payment method tokens, card brand, last four digits, wallet balance data, and transaction history for service operation and recordkeeping, but does not store full bank card numbers or full security codes in plain form on its own systems.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information only as necessary for service operation, including with:

  • Delivery drivers or logistics personnel, to complete pickup and drop-off services.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers, to process payments and wallet top-ups.
  • Technology vendors and hosting providers, to support app infrastructure, security, analytics, and communications.
  • Law enforcement, regulators, courts, or other authorized parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with legal requirements, and improve our operations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, business need, and applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information, subject to legal or operational limitations.
  • Withdraw permissions, such as location access, through your device settings.
  • Contact us regarding privacy questions, complaints, or requests.

9. Children’s Privacy

Mission Rush is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without appropriate authorization.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services, such as mapping, payment processing, crash reporting, analytics, messaging, or hosting services. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

11. International Use

If you use Mission Rush from outside your home jurisdiction, your information may be processed or stored in jurisdictions where we or our service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide notice through the app or other reasonable means.
